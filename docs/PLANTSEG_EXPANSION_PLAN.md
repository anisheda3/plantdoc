# Plan: PlantSeg expansion, realistic plants, still zoom leaf (started 10 Nov 2026)

The user asked for this on 10 Nov 2026: plants that look realistic and finished, every plant with diseases, using the two
major plant-disease datasets for their plants and diseases, and a fixed zoom leaf ("the leaf moves by itself"), with each
plant's own leaf. When asked, the user chose **PlantSeg, all of it** as the second dataset after PlantVillage. PlantDoc
labels are mapped too, because they cost nothing: every PlantDoc class is already a PlantVillage class.

This file tracks the work so it survives long sessions. Decisions go to `docs/DECISIONS.md` (69 and up).

## Datasets

| Dataset | Plants | Disease classes | Licence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PlantVillage (Hughes & Salathé 2015) | 14 | 26 (+ healthy) | CC0 (images used for leaves) | done (1 Nov) |
| PlantSeg (Wei et al. 2024, Zenodo) | 34 | 115 | CC BY 4.0 (dataset); images gathered from the web, URL per image in Metadata.csv | this plan |
| PlantDoc (Singh et al. 2020) | 13 | 17 (+ healthy) | CC BY 4.0 | label aliases only |

PlantSeg adds 20 hosts. Two of them merge into plants we already have: zucchini goes to squash (both *Cucurbita pepo*), and citrus goes to orange.
The other 18 are new: banana, basil, bean, broccoli, cabbage, carrot, cauliflower, celery, coffee, cucumber, eggplant, garlic, ginger, lettuce, maple, plum, rice, tobacco, wheat.
That is 18; with the 14 existing crops the app has 32 plants. Of PlantSeg's 115 classes, about 23 are diseases we already have; they get PlantSeg aliases. The other ~92 are new diseases.
